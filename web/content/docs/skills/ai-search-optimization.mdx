The AI Search Optimization Agent Skill checks how your site shows up in AI answers: Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT, Copilot, Perplexity, and Claude.

Google's own guidance says optimizing for its AI features is still SEO: they retrieve pages from the regular Search index. Your agent checks whether AI crawlers can reach your pages, whether the pages are eligible to be quoted, what AI answers already cite and send you, and whether your content is the kind worth citing.

## What this skill helps your agent do

- Check AI crawler access in robots.txt and through your firewall, crawler by crawler.
- Confirm key pages are indexed and allow snippets.
- Find the queries where Google's AI Overviews cite you.
- Measure visits from AI assistants and Copilot citations when those sources are connected.
- Read your key pages against the questions you want to be cited for.
- Recommend fixes in order: access, eligibility, then content.

## When to use it

Use this skill when you want to know whether AI assistants cite you, when AI traffic drops or never starts, or before investing in content for AI answers.

## What you get back

A report with the verdict, access and eligibility checks, what AI already cites and sends you, the questions that matter, and the pages to strengthen.

## How to get the best result

- List the questions or prompts you want to be cited for.
- Connect Umami, Bing Webmaster Tools, and Search Console so the agent can measure, not guess.
- Run an agent readiness scan first if you have never run one.

## Use it with OpenSEO MCP

Set up [OpenSEO MCP](/docs/mcp) so your agent can read agent readiness, AI Overview references, AI assistant traffic, and Copilot citations.
