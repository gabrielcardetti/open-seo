The Comparison Pages Agent Skill decides which comparison pages are worth building for your business and what each one needs.

People who search "X vs Y" or "X alternatives" are close to a decision. Your agent checks which comparison queries have demand, who ranks for them, and what your competitors' own pricing and feature pages say. It then plans each page with a fair verdict and a feature matrix where every competitor claim links to its source.

It also reviews comparison pages you already have.

## What this skill helps your agent do

- Build the list of candidate comparison queries from your competitors.
- Check demand and who ranks for each one.
- Collect competitor facts from their own pricing and feature pages, with dates.
- Plan each page's sections, matrix, verdict, title, and markup.
- Flag claims that still need a source or your confirmation.
- Review existing comparison pages and say what to fix.

## When to use it

Use this skill when buyers compare you with named competitors, when competitors already publish "vs" pages about you, or before building an alternatives section.

## What you get back

A report with the pages worth building, a plan for each one, the facts still to verify, and the first page to brief.

## How to get the best result

- Name the competitors buyers actually compare you with.
- Say honestly where each competitor is the better choice; the agent will keep the pages fair.
- Share sales notes on why customers switch, if you have them.
